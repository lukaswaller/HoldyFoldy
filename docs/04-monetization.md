# Holdyfoldy — Monetization Plan

*Research completed Oct 8, 2026.*

## Category standard

Generous free tracker → Pro at ~$5–15/mo ($60–150/yr). Paywalls gate: holding/portfolio limits, real-time data, advanced analytics, alerts, dividend tools, broker sync, exports.

| App | Free | Paid | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simply Wall St | 1 portfolio, 10 holdings, 5 reports/mo | $120/yr Premium · $180/yr Unlimited | limits, screeners, exports |
| Snowball Analytics | limited | ~$150/yr ($7.99/mo) | broker sync, portfolios, dividend planning |
| Yahoo Finance | best-in-class free | $35–70/mo tiers | research, 40-yr data, screeners, ad-free |
| Sharesight | 10 holdings | from ~$216/yr | tax reports, unlimited holdings |
| Delta | generous free | Pro subscription | unlimited portfolios, real-time, advanced metrics |
| Sway (indie) | 15 assets, 30-day history | Premium | history depth, asset count |

## Suggested Holdyfoldy ladder

### Free
- 1 portfolio, 15 holdings, **delayed quotes (15-min)**
- Basic charts, **privacy mode included** (make it the brand)
- CSV import

### Pro — ~$7.99/mo or $59.99/yr
Undercuts Snowball, sits under Simply Wall St. Three paywalled pillars:
1. **Real-time streaming quotes**
2. **Alerts** — price, % moves, dividend/ex-date, earnings reminders
3. **Tax & export** — FIFO lots, yearly summary, CSV/PDF export (Sharesight's wedge)

Plus: unlimited portfolios/holdings, dividend calendar + yield-on-cost, advanced views (relative/indexed, benchmark overlay).

### Launch promo — "Founding member" lifetime $149–199
Sway's "first 500 get Premium free 1 year" seeded reviews — copy that playbook for launch momentum.

## Rules

- Use Apple/Google in-app subscriptions (15–30% cut — price with that in mind); annual at ~40% discount is the norm.
- **Free = delayed, Pro = real-time** isn't just product design — real-time display licensing ($499+/mo) *requires* the revenue to fund it.
- No ads on the portfolio screen. Ever. (Finance + ads = trust death.)
