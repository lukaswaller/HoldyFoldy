# Holdyfoldy — Market and Competitor Research

*Research completed Oct 8, 2026.*

## Competitors

### My Stocks Portfolio & Market (the reference app)
- **Strengths:** Multi-portfolio, multi-currency conversion, streaming real-time US quotes with pre/post-market, per-stock fundamentals (P/E, EPS, beta, dividends), widgets, commission support, dark/light themes, **all data stored locally**, password lock.
- **Weaknesses (match the complaints):** no CSV import (every trade typed manually); ticker-format quirks; basic charts; dividends don't flow into the balance view — no single "all dividends earned" view.

### Yahoo Finance
- Best free tier anywhere: real-time US streaming, 5-yr financials, analyst ratings, earnings calendar, 100+ exchanges.
- **Gaps:** portfolio features are basic; aggressive ads; no discreet display mode; tracking is secondary.

### Sharesight
- The "grown-up" tracker: ~700K instruments across 40 markets, automatic dividend tracking, best-in-class tax reporting, corporate actions, multi-currency with FX gains. Free up to 10 holdings; paid from ~$216/yr.
- **Gaps:** web/desktop-centric, thin mobile app; functional-not-beautiful.

### Snowball Analytics (~$150/yr)
- Dividend-first: dividend calendar, yield-on-cost, payout planning; native rebalancing with drift; broker sync via Plaid/SnapTrade; modern UI.
- **Gaps:** no trade execution; weaker on crypto.

### Delta Investment Tracker
- Best all-rounder mobile UX (stocks + crypto + NFTs); broker/exchange connect; curated news; generous free tier. Rated "best overall" in 2026 roundups.
- **Gaps:** no rebalancing tools; shallow analysis.

### Simply Wall St (Free / $120/yr / $180/yr)
- Visual company analysis ("Snowflake" scores), 120K stocks across 90 markets, 4.6★ mobile-first.
- **Gaps:** no real-time P&L depth; very limited free tier.

### Stock Events
- Dividend-investor favorite: calendar, dividend growth, screener, ex-div/earnings alerts. Clean mobile UX.

## The privacy-mode gap — the genuine differentiator

Almost no mainstream tracker has a real "hide my money" mode:
- **Ghostfolio** (open-source): **"Zen Mode"** — one click hides all sensitive numbers for discreet screen sharing.
- **Interactive Brokers Mobile**: tap a sensitive field to hide it, tap again to reveal.
- **Sway** (indie): privacy mode + PIN lock + encrypted data.
- Yahoo, Delta, Simply Wall St, Snowball: **no documented discreet mode. Nobody big owns this.**

## Positioning

Holdyfoldy = Delta-grade mobile UX + Sharesight-grade accounting (FIFO lots, dividends, tax reports) + a **privacy-first brand** (Zen-style mode as a headline feature, not a buried setting) + the subset/lens viewing My Stocks fumbles.
